# Northwind Labs expense policy (v1)

Northwind Labs is a fictional company. All amounts are in USD. Expenses in other currencies are
converted at the exchange rate on the receipt date.

## General

1. Every expense needs an itemised receipt showing the merchant, date and total.
2. Receipts must be submitted within **90 days** of the purchase date. Older receipts are rejected.
3. A receipt that has already been submitted (same merchant, date and total) is a duplicate and is
   rejected.
4. If the total or date can't be read, the employee is asked for a clearer copy.
5. Any single expense over **$1,000** needs approval from Finance.

## Categories and limits

| Category | Employee | Manager / Finance / IT / People Ops | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meals | $75 per day | $120 per day | Tax and tips up to 20% count towards the limit. |
| Travel (taxi, rideshare, train, flights) | No limit | No limit | Flights over $800 need manager approval. |
| Lodging | $300 per night | $300 per night | |
| Software and subscriptions | $50 per month | $50 per month | Above that needs manager approval. |
| Office supplies | $100 | $100 | |
| Equipment (hardware) | — | — | Anything over $500 needs IT approval. |
| Other | — | — | Always needs manager approval. |

Amounts over the meal, lodging and office supply limits are not reimbursed; the rest is.

## Never reimbursed

- Alcohol. Alcohol line items (and their share of tax and tip) are removed; the rest of the
  receipt can still be reimbursed.
- Personal items, gifts to yourself, fines and parking tickets.
- Tips above 20% of the pre-tax subtotal (the excess is removed).

## Approvals

- Approvals go to the employee's manager, unless the policy names another team (Finance, IT).
- Nobody approves their own expense. A manager's own expenses go to their manager, or to Finance
  if they have none.
